# SevenTwo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 2026_

SevenTwo is a personal project for organizing live Texas Hold'em home games. It helps poker groups plan games, manage sessions, and keep shared history. It does not run online poker games.

## Data SevenTwo may process

Depending on how you use the app, SevenTwo may store:

- Your username or display name and email address
- Your workspace membership and role
- Your poker nickname
- Session participation and history
- Buy-in, rebuy, cash-out, and poker result information
- Plan and Poll availability responses
- Notification preferences and device push-subscription details

Workspace owners may also add **UNREGISTERED** players. These players do not have SevenTwo accounts, but limited nickname, session, and poker-result data may be stored as part of the workspace's shared records.

## Why this data is used

The data is used only to provide SevenTwo's core features: account access, workspace management, live poker-session management, planning and voting, notifications, and player statistics and history.

SevenTwo does not process card payments, hold player funds, or facilitate real-money online poker. Cash and Card labels record how a home-game group handled money outside the app.

## Service providers

SevenTwo uses:

- **Supabase** for accounts and stored application data
- **Cloudflare Pages** to host and deliver the web application

These providers may process normal technical request information under their own privacy terms.

## Retention and your choices

Data is generally retained while an account or workspace remains in use. Shared session history may remain until it is deleted by an authorized workspace member. Provider backups and technical logs may persist for a limited period after deletion.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data where applicable. For workspace-managed records, contact the workspace owner first. For account or project-level requests, contact the project maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/karthuscode). Do not include passwords or other sensitive information in a public issue.

## Security

SevenTwo uses account authentication, workspace permissions, and managed infrastructure to limit access to workspace data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so access codes and account credentials should be shared and stored carefully.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as SevenTwo evolves. Material changes will be reflected in this file with an updated date.
